Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a hotel on StayFinder?

Simply enter your destination, check-in and check-out dates, and number of guests in the search box. You'll see real-time results from over 8 million hotels worldwide. Choose your favorite and book securely.

Is booking on StayFinder safe?

Yes, absolutely. All bookings are processed through our trusted partner network using secure, encrypted payment systems. Your personal and payment data is always protected.

Do I really get 5% cash back?

Yes! Every completed booking through StayFinder earns you approximately 5% cash back, credited to your account after your stay. Plus, 1 tonne of CO₂ is offset on your behalf — at no extra cost.

Can I cancel or modify my booking?

Cancellation and modification policies depend on the specific hotel and room type you selected. Free cancellation is available on most stays — you'll see the exact policy before confirming your booking.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to search. However, creating a free account lets you track bookings, manage rewards, and get personalized deals.

Which countries are supported?

StayFinder supports hotels in over 190 countries, including the USA, UK, Europe, Asia, Middle East, and beyond.
